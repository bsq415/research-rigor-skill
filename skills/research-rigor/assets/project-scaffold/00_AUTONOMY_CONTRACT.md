`guided` means advance only when the researcher requests the next unit of work.
`full-cycle` means continue through every reversible, authorized, evidence-justified
step without asking for routine micro-decisions. Both modes remain human-led.

## Actions the assistant may perform

- [ ] Inspect authorized local files, code, data summaries, and prior artifacts.
- [ ] Search permitted sources and build source-anchored literature records.
- [ ] Propose, compare, reject, and rank research-question candidates.
- [ ] Design protocols, tests, baselines, ablations, and statistical checks.
- [ ] Implement and execute work within the stated resource and privacy limits.
- [ ] Diagnose engineering failures and retry only under the frozen protocol.
- [ ] Audit results, downgrade unsupported claims, and recommend redesign or stop.
- [ ] Draft and revise text, tables, and figures from verified evidence.
- [ ] Run structural, reproducibility, privacy, and manuscript audits.

## Actions reserved for an authorized human

- [ ] Final question, claim, interpretation, authorship, and submission decisions.
- [ ] Ethics, privacy, license, disclosure, and external-release authorization.
- [ ] Uploading unpublished material to external services or portals.
- [ ] Purchasing resources, accepting material cost, or changing production systems.
- [ ] Approving a protocol amendment after confirmatory results were inspected.
- [ ] Destructive cleanup or deletion of material research artifacts.

## Acceptance

- [ ] The operating mode and authorized scope are explicit.
- [ ] Resource, privacy, and external-action limits are explicit.
- [ ] Human-only decisions cannot be inferred from silence.
- [ ] Failed or null scientific results will be preserved, not cosmetically repaired.
- [ ] The researcher understands that the workflow cannot guarantee novelty, validity, or acceptance.
