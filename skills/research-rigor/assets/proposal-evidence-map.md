# Proposal evidence map

Optional working aid. Reuse existing notes instead when they already carry these
links. This record is normally private; do not publish an applicant's source
documents, unpublished questions or personal details as a skill example.

## Scientific chain

For each central question, record:

- Decision need, observed failure and mechanism:
- Closest competing answer, its source anchor and strongest already-done argument:
- Bounded gap, search scope/cutoff and remaining uncertainty:
- RQ and mechanism-based hypothesis, if applicable:
- Proposed operation; reused components; available information; feasible set:
- Decisive baseline and matched acquisition/data/tuning/compute budgets:
- Metric, decision unit, population, uncertainty and falsifier:
- Evidence needed; current status: established, preliminary, proposed or unresolved:
- Work-package input/output, dependency, milestone and resource requirement:
- Failure trigger, worthwhile fallback and optional extension:
- Verified applicant capability and skill/resource still to acquire:

## Guarantee and cost contract, if relevant

State the controlled quantity, randomness, conditioning and assumptions. Account
for group/simultaneous claims, data dependence, model selection and field transfer.
Separate minimum sample count, useful tightness and acquisition time; separate
inference runtime, physical action and end-to-end latency. Identify where real
labels or deployment data are necessary and whether they are actually available.

## Revision and handoff

| Issue/source | Verified verdict | Change and locations | Evidence/check | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

List dependent sections to revisit when a central claim changes. Record final
render/compile and page checks, citation checks, unresolved reading and the
canonical editable and rendered outputs. Do not label an unexecuted experiment
or planned study as completed validation.
