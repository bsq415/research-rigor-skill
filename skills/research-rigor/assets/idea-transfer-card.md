# Literature-to-idea card

Use in existing notes or copy when a persistent record helps. Replace prompts
with source-linked content; leave unknown facts explicitly unresolved.

## Source roles and evidence

| Source/version/page or equation | Role: problem, method, or near-neighbor | Relevant mechanism or operation | Assumptions |
|---|---|---|---|

Inspiration provenance: researcher-stated, documented by a historical record,
or analyst reconstruction. Record historical date and current search cutoff
separately when they matter.

## Bridge

One sentence connecting the new control/need to the reusable operation and its
decision value:

| Old object | New counterpart | What survives | What changes and why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|

Transformation labels and conditions: exact, conditional, bound, relaxation,
approximation, or heuristic. State bound direction and target checks.

Cheapest decisive check, expected interpretation of either outcome, and resource
budget. Distinguish planned from executed work:
